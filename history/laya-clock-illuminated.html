How to read the clock

The outer ring has fixed tala spokes: the current akshara illuminates in teal. The inner ring has fixed subdivision spokes: the current nadai pulse illuminates in amber. At samam, the first tala spoke illuminates. An offset shifts the amber highlight relative to the tala without moving samam. In this prototype “1 thalli” means one subdivision, not one akshara.

This is a teaching metronome, not a full korvai engine. The speed multiplier increases the number of sounded pulses per akshara; it is not a complete model of kalai, which also concerns tala execution. Audio uses synthesized clicks rather than sampled mridangam strokes.
