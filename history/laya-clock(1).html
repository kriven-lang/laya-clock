How to read the clock

The teal hour hand makes one full turn per tala cycle. The amber minute hand makes one full turn per akshara. Each click on the inner circle is a nadai subdivision. The top marker denotes samam. An offset moves the amber phrase reference relative to the tala; it does not change where samam is. In this prototype “1 thalli” means one subdivision, not one akshara.

This is a teaching metronome, not a full korvai engine. The speed multiplier increases the number of sounded pulses per akshara; it is not a complete model of kalai, which also concerns tala execution. Audio uses synthesized clicks rather than sampled mridangam strokes.
